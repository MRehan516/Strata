01 — Field notesWhy layers

Most tools track deliverables. Strata tracks the decisions underneath them — the part that actually gets lost.

A project isn’t a folder; it’s a sequence of judgement calls. Specs get overwritten, prototypes fork into oblivion, and the reasoning behind “final_v3_REAL” dies the moment its author leaves the team. Strata never overwrites: every state of a project stays a layer you can return to, diff, and sign.

The ledger lives on a server, so it follows you across devices and updates live for everyone in the room. And because it’s exposed over WebMCP, your agent isn’t a chat sidebar guessing at your UI — it holds the same structured handle on project truth that you do.

02 — CapabilitiesEach one is an agent tool too

Every spec, decision and prototype is an append-only layer with an ID you can cite in a meeting — or hand to your agent. Stored server-side; survives closed tabs, other browsers, other devices.

Diff any two layers field by field — in the UI for you, as structured data for your agent. The generated diagram renders the whole strata as a downloadable figure.

Approvals are recorded against the exact layer they refer to — named, timestamped, validated on the server. Agents can prepare and record, but only on a named human’s instruction.

Bundle approved layers into release notes — exportable as Markdown. Your agent drafts from the ledger; you approve the cut. Export the whole workspace as JSON or CSV anytime.

04 — Method

Four moves, repeated until the thing ships. Each one writes to the same ledger — for you, for your teammates in other windows, and for your agent.

i.

Capture

Record specs, decisions, prototypes. Each becomes a layer with provenance from minute zero — by hand, or narrated to your agent.

ii.

Compose

Assemble a stratum: the exact bundle of versions that constitutes this attempt at the truth. One handle, one state, no ambiguity.

iii.

Critique

Reviewers sign inside the layer they’re commenting on. Your agent can pre-check that a layer contradicts no earlier decision before a human ever looks.

iv.

Cut

When contested layers resolve, cut the release. The agent drafts the notes from the ledger; the ledger seals the record.

Getting started — 5 moves

  1. ✓Open a workspace — create one (optionally password-locked) or join with a 6-character code.
  2. ✓Add your first layer — a spec, decision, prototype or release, with the big form below-left.
  3. ✓Sign one off — type the reviewer’s name on a layer card and approve or reject it.
  4. ✓Export something — a diagram, release notes, JSON or CSV.
  5. ✓Let your agent act — open the Agent console above, copy the URL into ChatGPT (or use flagged Chrome), paste a ready-made prompt, and watch the tool log fill. The agent can’t run inside this tab — it lives in the agent’s own chat.
